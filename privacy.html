Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This policy describes how The Funky Monkeys uses information through TFM Chat Digest.

Information accessed

The tool accesses Google Chat conversation information, membership information and message content available to the authorised account. This may include names and personal information contained in those conversations.

Purpose and processing

Information is used to create internal digests and support coordination at The Funky Monkeys. Digests are stored in Google Docs. Processing checkpoints, which may include message content, are stored in Google Apps Script.

Google provides the underlying Chat, document storage and script services. Digest content is also made available to OpenAI’s ChatGPT service for internal briefings and action lists. This processing is subject to the applicable service terms and account settings.

The Funky Monkeys does not sell this information or use it for advertising. Private messages and digests are not published on this website.

Retention and access

The current digest is replaced when a new successful digest is generated. Older document versions, historical outputs and processing records may remain until separately removed. Revoking authorisation does not automatically delete stored information or copies already provided to ChatGPT.

Authorisation can be revoked through the connected Google account’s third-party access settings. This stops future access by the tool.

Questions and requests

To ask about access, correction or deletion of information, contact tfm.meme@gmail.com.
